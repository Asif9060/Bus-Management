❓ FAQs

How do I book a ticket?

Select your route, choose a bus, pick your seats, enter passenger details, and confirm your booking.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, you can cancel up to 2 hours before departure. Refund will be processed within 3-5 business days.

How do I get my ticket?

After booking, you'll receive an e-ticket via SMS and email. Show the ticket at the counter or on your phone.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, credit/debit cards, and cash at partner counters.
